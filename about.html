About

I studied Graphic Design at Western Connecticut State University, where I earned my Bachelor of Arts degree. I was also heavily involved with WCSU's radio station, WXCI 91.7. My involvement with WXCI 91.7 led me to receive the coveted WXCI Award of Excellence. I learned many skills at WCSU that I continue to use to this day.


I discovered my passion for web design mostly through freelancing. During my education at WCSU one of the very few things they did not have was a web design program. In order to stay relevant in the digital age I was forced to learn this on my own. The experience of teaching myself about this rapidly-evolving industry has been both challenging and extremely rewarding.


Besides freelancing for various individuals and businesses, I have spent the greater part of my career working in radio, most of which at Clear Channel Media + Entertainment. I have had the distinct pleasure of working in virtually every department ranging from Information Technology, Programming, to Integrated Media.

I don't bite. If you need a design consultation, email me.

I look forward to hearing from you!
