About

I’m a digital and technical professional with a background in graphic design, web development, digital media, and technology. I enjoy figuring out how things work, solving problems, and creating digital experiences that are both functional and easy to use.

My interest in the web began while studying Graphic Design at Western Connecticut State University, where I earned my Bachelor of Arts degree. At the time, WCSU didn’t offer a dedicated web design program, so I taught myself. I started learning HTML and CSS, experimenting with website design and development, and eventually began taking on freelance projects for individuals and businesses. Teaching myself web development sparked an interest that has stayed with me ever since.

My professional career has given me the opportunity to build on that foundation in a much larger digital environment. I spent more than two decades with iHeartMedia, working across areas including Information Technology, Programming, Integrated Media, and Digital Operations. Most recently, I worked in Digital Support, where I supported SaaS platforms, proprietary CMS tools, APIs, third-party integrations, and the technology behind more than 900 digital properties.

That experience gave me a perspective that goes beyond simply designing a website. I understand the importance of what happens behind the scenes—how websites are built and maintained, how content and digital assets are managed, how different systems connect, and how technical issues can affect the people using them.

Today, I continue to combine my creative and technical backgrounds through web design, development, digital operations, and problem-solving. I’m constantly learning new technologies and looking for better ways to build, improve, and support digital experiences.

This portfolio is a collection of some of the projects, skills, and ideas I’ve developed along the way. It’s also a place for me to experiment, keep learning, and build something new.

Have an idea, a project, or a problem that needs solving? Let’s talk.
